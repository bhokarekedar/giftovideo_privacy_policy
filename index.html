Privacy Policy for GIF to Video: Reel Meme Maker

Last updated: October 2026

"GIF to Video: Reel Meme Maker" ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Our application operates entirely offline and on-device:

  • We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • We do not require account registration or login.
  • We do not track your location or usage habits.

2. Media Files and Permissions

  • The app requires access to your device's photo library/storage solely to allow you to import GIFs or images and export the rendered video files back to your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or analytics services.

4. Children's Privacy

Because our app does not collect any personal data, it does not knowingly collect or solicit information from children under the age of 13.
